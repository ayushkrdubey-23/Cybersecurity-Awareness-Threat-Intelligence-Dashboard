Project Title
Cybersecurity Awareness & Threat Intelligence Dashboard
Objective
Build a unified dashboard that ingests threat-intel feeds (IOCs, CVEs, trending malware TTPs) and internal signals, normalizes and enriches them, scores organizational risk, and delivers bite-sized awareness content and remediation tasks to users and admins.
Industry Relevance
Modern SOCs and IT teams blend external threat intelligence with internal telemetry for faster detection and better hygiene. Pairing actionable IOCs/CVEs with awareness nudges reduces phishing clicks, patch delay, and incident response time across enterprises, universities, and SMBs.
